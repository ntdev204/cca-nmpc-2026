## Relevance and boundary

SCU-T-MPC is a nearby risk-aware planning comparator with uncertainty and
qualitative failure analysis. It does not establish the project's fixed-budget
context allocator, position-state Mecanum plant with body-velocity commands or current-snapshot LSTM
interface.

## Consequence for the gap

The gap must remain empirical and matched: fixed clearance, fixed global path,
explicit local-path trigger, controller baselines and separate safety/tracking/
runtime outcomes. A generic claim that risk-aware MPC or social uncertainty is
new is not admissible.
