# A unified local risk map for uncertainty-aware mobile-robot navigation (2026)

## Bibliographic verification

- The [Sensors publisher record](https://doi.org/10.3390/s26123900) identifies
  the 2026 article *A Unified Local Risk Map for Uncertainty-Aware Mobile Robot
  Navigation in Cluttered and Dynamic Environments* and DOI
  `10.3390/s26123900`.
- The publisher abstract describes a continuous local risk representation that
  combines contextual and uncertainty sources for dynamic navigation. Detailed
  Zotero admission, full-text extraction and correction/retraction checks remain
  open.

## Relevance and boundary

The record is a nearby risk-representation prior, not evidence that a
fixed-budget CCA allocator is novel. It strengthens the requirement to compare
context-off, uncertainty-only and distance-only controls under the same map,
clearance and failure accounting.

## Consequence for the gap and experiments

The remaining gap must be stated as a bounded empirical comparison of the
declared CCA interface inside position-state Mecanum NMPC with body-velocity
commands. A continuous risk
map, uncertainty-aware navigation or generic real-time safety claim cannot be
used as a standalone contribution.
