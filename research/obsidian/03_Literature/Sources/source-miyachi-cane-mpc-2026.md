# Miyachi et al. 2026 — dynamic cost maps and MPC for an accompanying robot

Primary record: [ROBOMECH Journal](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40648-026-00345-6).

## Verified scope

The open publisher article describes a dynamic cost-map target-selection
method and an MPC velocity-command generator for an accompanying cane-type
robot. The paper reports experiments in a living lab and with a real robot for
dynamic-object avoidance and smooth accompaniment.

## Boundary for this project

This is adjacent real-robot MPC and dynamic-obstacle evidence. It does not
establish CCA fixed-budget allocation, LSTM-in-CCA context semantics or a
position-state Mecanum plant with body-velocity commands. It reinforces the need to separate the global goal,
local target/path update, physical clearance, task completion and human-facing
qualitative measures. It remains screening-only and is not a final citation.
