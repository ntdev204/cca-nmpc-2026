# Pham and Han (2026) — Hybrid Mecanum control architecture

## Bibliographic verification

- [SAGE record](https://doi.org/10.1177/18758967251394861) identifies the
  Journal of Intelligent & Fuzzy Systems article, volume 50, issue 6,
  pages 1923--1958 and DOI `10.1177/18758967251394861`.
- The abstract describes an SMO--MPC--PID--fuzzy hybrid architecture with
  wheel-slip compensation, MATLAB/Simulink evaluation and real-parameter
  simulation. Zotero admission and correction/retraction checks remain open.
- The publisher record was rechecked on 2026-08-14; its reported validation is
  still a platform/control-architecture boundary, not evidence for the present
  CCA claim.

## Relevance and boundary

This is a platform/control-architecture boundary prior. It reports tracking,
energy, settling, disturbance and parameter-uncertainty outcomes, but it does
not provide a human-aware context allocator, current-snapshot LSTM interface or
the project's matched MPC/DWA/MPPI benchmark.

## Consequence for the benchmark

The CCA study must keep Mecanum platform novelty separate from the allocator and
report actuator limits, tracking error, control activity and uncertainty
conditions. The publisher record also states that the reported validation is
simulation-restricted; its abstract-level performance numbers are not reused as
baseline results here. The source is not evidence for superiority or for the
present gap.
