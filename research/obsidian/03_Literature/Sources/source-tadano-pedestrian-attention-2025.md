## Relevance and boundary

The project page describes attention-state estimation, uncertainty-aware
prediction and cost-map adaptation, with an indoor modified wheelchair
experiment. This occupies the attention-aware prediction/cost-map space. It
does not establish the present fixed-budget CCA allocation, position-state
Mecanum CCA-NMPC interface, or the no-human-trajectory-overlay contract.

The record is therefore a comparator and gap-boundary source only. It must not
be used as a verified citation or as evidence that CCA-NMPC is superior.
