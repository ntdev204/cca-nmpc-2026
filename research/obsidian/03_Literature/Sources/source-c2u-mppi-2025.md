# C2U-MPPI — chance-constrained sampling MPC

## Record

- **Title:** *Chance-Constrained Sampling-Based MPC for Collision Avoidance in Uncertain Dynamic Environments*
- **Venue:** IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, vol. 10, no. 7, 2025
- **Publisher record:** [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11021391)
- **Evidence level:** publisher abstract/metadata only; IEEE full text and exact Zotero metadata are not yet reconciled. Zotero local exact-title search on 2026-08-14 returned zero items.

## Relevance to the research gap

The record places chance constraints directly inside an unscented sampling-based
MPPI controller and reports simulated and real-world evaluation in dynamic
human-shared environments. This closes any broad claim that probabilistic
collision handling or real-world risk-aware MPPI is absent from the literature.
It also strengthens the requirement to include MPPI as a matched baseline with
the same map, state/command interface, timing budget and failure denominator.

## Boundary for this project

C2U-MPPI is a controller comparator, not evidence that a fixed-total CCA
allocation is novel. Its sampling-based chance constraints, uncertainty
representation and platform must be extracted from the full paper before any
methodological comparison. No numerical value from the abstract is copied into
the claim register or manuscript.
