# Alfayizi (2026) — Traction-adaptive Mecanum tracking benchmark

## Bibliographic verification

- [Publisher full text](https://www.iieta.org/journals/jesa/paper/10.18280/jesa.590406)
  identifies the 2026 open-access article, pages 925--941, DOI
  `10.18280/jesa.590406`.
- The publisher page reports five Mecanum tracking scenarios and a comparison
  between traction-adaptive super-twisting control and PID under wheel-speed
  constraints. Zotero admission and correction/retraction checks remain open.

## Method and evidence

The article models traction variation, actuator limits and wheel-speed
feasibility, and reports a robustness--terminal-precision trade-off across
curved tracking, friction, payload and slip-shock scenarios. It explicitly
reports tracking error, terminal error, saturation and control activity.

## Consequence for the gap and benchmark

Mecanum dynamics, actuator feasibility and tracking benchmarks are established
prior art. The CCA study must retain torque/wheel feasibility, control-variation
and terminal-error metrics and must not treat the Mecanum platform alone as
algorithmic novelty. This is a platform/evaluation comparator, not a human-aware
risk-allocation comparator.
