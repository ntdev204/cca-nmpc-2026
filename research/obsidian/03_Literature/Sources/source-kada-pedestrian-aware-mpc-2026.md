# Kada et al. 2026 — pedestrian-aware MPC and hesitation

Primary record: [Journal of the Robotics Society of Japan](https://doi.org/10.7210/jrsj.44.196).

## Verified scope

The publisher record describes a model-predictive behavior-planning method
that minimizes a decision-entropy measure associated with pedestrian hesitation
in crossing scenarios. It reports real-world AMR--pedestrian experiments with
384 crossing scenarios and six participants, and evaluates perceived clarity,
safety, cooperativeness and smoothness.

## Boundary for this project

This is a human-centered MPC and qualitative-evaluation boundary, not evidence
for a fixed-budget CCA allocator, LSTM context interface or position-state
Mecanum NMPC. It strengthens PR40's requirement to separate physical safety,
tracking and human-centered qualitative outcomes. It remains screening-only;
the record has not been imported into the frozen Zotero export.
