## Relevance and boundary

The paper reports robot–pedestrian interaction data collected with overhead
video, multiple robot platforms and moving/stationary/no-robot conditions. It
shows why robot type and interaction condition must be recorded when evaluating
human response. The dataset is not the supplied Mecanum robot, does not provide
Astra-S/N10P provenance for this project, and its data-access/license terms
were not verified here.

This source strengthens the protocol requirement for scene-, episode- and
robot-condition metadata. It does not justify importing an external trajectory
dataset as target evidence, nor does it change the rule that only CCA-NMPC may
retain a causal human-position prediction internally.
