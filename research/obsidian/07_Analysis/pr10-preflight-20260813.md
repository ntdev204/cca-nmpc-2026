# PR10 preflight — candidate boundary, 2026-08-14

The previous ten-image Internet cohort, blind annotation manifest and
mechanical preflight JSON were deleted before the new campaign. Their paths,
sizes and hashes are recorded only in
[[07_Analysis/development-artifact-purge-20260813]]; no old PASS result is
active evidence.

## New acquisition gate

The next cohort must be licensed and downloaded under a new acquisition ID.
Before detector inference, the validator must recheck schema, local byte
hashes, decoding and dimensions, annotation-to-manifest binding, bounding-box
bounds, source-group disjointness, real/non-AI media, rights and privacy. A
second blinded annotator and an independent adjudicator are required; the
pretraining-overlap review and held-out ID/OOD expansion must be recorded before
the tool version or threshold is frozen.

The in-memory contract fixtures remain software checks only. They are not image
labels and cannot admit a cohort. YOLO26s-pose remains a measurement tool, not
the control contribution; the image branch records current position, speed,
direction, confidence and validity only.

The current seven-image candidate cohort is recorded in
[[07_Analysis/web-cohort-acquisition-20260814]] and the external checkpoint in
[[07_Analysis/yolo26s-pose-candidate-acquisition-20260814]]. Neither is
admitted evidence.
