# Project map — CCA--NMPC + LSTM

Visual entry point: [[00_MOC/knowledge-galaxy]].

This is the hub of the research knowledge graph. The reasoning chain is
`source -> gap -> theory -> method -> decision`; experimental outputs are
deliberately outside the vault.

## Spine

1. [[03_Literature/literature-review]] and [[03_Literature/source-index]] identify prior art.
2. [[04_Research_Gap/research-gap]] states the narrow, falsifiable gap.
3. [[05_Theory/system-model]] and [[05_Theory/context-aware-risk-allocation]] define the model.
4. [[05_Theory/theorems]] and [[05_Theory/proofs]] record mathematical obligations.
5. [[06_Methods/methods-overview]] binds dataset, perception, LSTM, map, and control protocols.
6. [[08_Decisions/decision-register]] records scope decisions, including the
   [[08_Decisions/position-state-control-scope-20260813]] amendment.
